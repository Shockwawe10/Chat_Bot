# What to provide for a quote

To prepare a commercial offer, the company needs the following information.

## For every project

- Company name and a contact person with an email address.
- Site location: address or GPS coordinates.
- Planned plant size in kWp, or the available area in m².
- Mounting type: ground, flat roof, pitched roof or carport.
- The PV module model, if it is already chosen. If not, the company designs for a typical module size and checks it again later.
- The desired start of installation.

## Extra for roof projects

- Roof type and covering, for example concrete slab with membrane, or metal sheet.
- Roof slope in degrees.
- Age of the building and of the waterproofing.
- Roof drawings or a structural report, if available.

## Extra for ground-mount projects

- Land plot boundaries.
- Any existing soil reports.
- Known underground cables or pipes.

## What happens next

A request with this information gets a reply within 2 working days and an offer to book a site survey. Missing information can be collected during the survey, but it can delay the commercial offer.
