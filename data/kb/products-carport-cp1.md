# CarportFrame CP-1 solar carport

CP-1 is a steel carport whose roof is made of PV modules. It covers one row of parking spaces, or two rows when two carports are placed back to back.

## Specifications

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Material | Steel, hot-dip galvanised |
| Coating thickness | 85 µm average |
| Corrosivity category | C4 |
| Tilt angle | 5° to 10° |
| Clear height | 2.5 m |
| Depth | 5.0 m per row of parking spaces |
| Maximum wind speed | 30 m/s |
| Maximum snow load | 2.4 kN/m² |
| Foundation | Concrete |

## Options

- Rain gutter along the low edge.
- Mounting points for EV chargers on the columns. The company does not supply or connect the chargers.
- Impact protection at the base of each column.
