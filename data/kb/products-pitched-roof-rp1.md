# RoofPitch RP-1 pitched-roof system

RP-1 is a rail system for sloped roofs. Modules are mounted parallel to the roof surface.

## Specifications

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Material | Aluminium rails, anodised |
| Coating thickness | 15 µm anodic layer |
| Fasteners and hooks | Stainless steel A2 |
| Corrosivity category | C3 |
| Roof slope | 10° to 60° |
| Maximum wind speed | 30 m/s |
| Maximum snow load | 3.0 kN/m² |

## Roof coverings

- Clay and concrete tiles: roof hooks fixed to the rafters.
- Trapezoidal metal sheet: short rails fixed directly to the sheet.
- Standing seam metal: clamps on the seams, with no holes in the roof.

RP-1 is not used on roofs covered with asbestos-cement sheets. The company does not work on such roofs.
