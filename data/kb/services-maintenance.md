# Inspection and maintenance

## Recommended inspections

- A regular inspection every 12 months.
- An extra inspection after a storm with wind gusts above 25 m/s or after unusually heavy snow.

## What an inspection covers

- Bolt torque on a sample of connections.
- Coating condition: scratches, white rust and red rust.
- Post and pile movement on ground-mount plants.
- Ballast position on flat roofs.
- Module clamps.

## Repairs

Small coating damage is repaired with zinc-rich paint during the inspection. Damaged parts are replaced after the customer approves a repair offer.

Regular inspections are a condition of the warranty. See the warranty document.
