# Warranty

## What is covered

| Warranty | Period | Covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product warranty | 15 years | Manufacturing defects in the mounting system and coating failure (red rust on more than 5% of a part's surface) |
| Installation warranty | 5 years | Installation work done by the company's crews |

Both periods start on the date of the handover certificate.

## Conditions

The warranty applies only if all of these are true:

- The system was built to the company's approved design.
- The corrosivity category of the site is not higher than the category the product is rated for.
- An inspection was done every 12 months, by the company or a contractor approved by the company, and the inspection reports are kept.
- The system was not modified without written approval from the company.

## What is not covered

- PV modules, inverters and cables.
- Damage from wind or snow above the values used in the structural calculation.
- Damage from vehicles, vandalism, fire or earthworks near the posts.

## How to make a claim

Send the project number, a description and photos to support@example.com. The company replies within 5 working days and arranges an inspection if needed.
