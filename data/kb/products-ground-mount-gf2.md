# GroundFrame GF-2 twin-post ground-mount system

GF-2 is a ground-mount system with two rows of posts per table. It carries larger tables and higher loads than GF-1 and is made for aggressive environments.

## Specifications

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Material | Steel with zinc-magnesium coating |
| Coating thickness | 25 µm per side |
| Corrosivity category | C5 |
| Module layout | 3 modules in portrait per table row |
| Tilt angle | 10° to 30° |
| Maximum wind speed | 36 m/s |
| Maximum snow load | 3.5 kN/m² |
| Ground clearance | At least 0.6 m |
| Foundation | Rammed posts or screw piles |
| Typical plant size | 1 MWp to 100 MWp |

## Where it is used

- Coastal sites within 10 km of the sea.
- Sites near chemical plants, fertiliser storage or other sources of aggressive air.
- Sites with high snow loads, up to 3.5 kN/m².

## Thin but stronger coating

The zinc-magnesium coating on GF-2 is thinner than the hot-dip galvanising on GF-1, but it protects better in salty air. Coating thickness alone does not show which system resists corrosion better; the corrosivity category does.

## Terrain

GF-2 can follow terrain slopes of up to 10% in the north-south direction without earthworks.
