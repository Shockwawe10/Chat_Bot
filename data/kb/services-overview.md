# Services overview

Example Solar Ltd. offers four services. A customer can order all of them as one package or only some of them.

| Service | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| Design | Layout, structural calculation, drawings and bill of materials |
| Supply | Mounting structures, fasteners and foundation parts delivered to the site |
| Installation | Building the mounting system on site and fixing the modules to it |
| Maintenance | Periodic inspection and repair of installed mounting systems |

Supply without design is not possible: every system the company supplies is calculated for the specific site.

Installation is offered only for mounting systems that the company also supplied.

Maintenance is offered for any mounting system the company supplied, also when another contractor installed it.
