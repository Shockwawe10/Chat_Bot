# Supply and delivery

## Production

Mounting system parts are made by partner factories to the company's drawings. Every batch is checked on arrival at the company's warehouse: dimensions, coating thickness and fastener certificates.

## Delivery

- Parts are delivered by truck, packed by table row or roof field, with a packing list on every pallet.
- Delivery to the site is included in every supply order.
- The customer provides a flat, dry storage area of about 200 m² for each MWp of a ground-mount plant.
- For roof projects, the customer provides a way to lift the parts onto the roof, unless the installation is also ordered from the company.

## Typical duration

Supply usually takes 6 to 10 weeks after the customer approves the detailed design. This is a typical range, not a commitment. The delivery dates for a specific project are set in the contract.
