# Installation

The company's own crews build the mounting system and fix the PV modules to it. Electrical work is not included.

## What the customer provides

- Access to the site for trucks and machines.
- A storage area for the parts (see the document about supply and delivery).
- Electricity and water on site for tools and workers.
- The PV modules, delivered to the site before module mounting starts.

## Ground-mount installation

Posts are driven by the company's ramming machines. A typical crew installs the structure for up to 1 MWp per week on a prepared site. Stony soil, frozen ground and bad weather make it slower.

## Roof installation

Roof work starts only after the roof assessment is approved. Crews use fall protection on every roof. Work stops when the wind speed is above 12 m/s.

## Handover

After installation the company checks the torque of a sample of bolts, makes a photo report and signs a handover certificate with the customer.

Installation times above are typical values, not a commitment. The dates for a specific project are set in the contract.
