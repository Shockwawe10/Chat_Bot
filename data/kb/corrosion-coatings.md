# Coatings and materials

## Steel systems

- **Hot-dip galvanising.** Used on GroundFrame GF-1 and CarportFrame CP-1. Parts are galvanised after cutting and drilling, so the edges and holes are also protected. Average coating thickness: 85 µm.
- **Zinc-magnesium coating.** Used on GroundFrame GF-2. The coating is applied to the steel strip before forming. Cut edges are protected by the coating itself, which flows over them as it corrodes slowly. Coating thickness: 25 µm per side.

Bolts and nuts on steel systems are hot-dip galvanised.

## Aluminium systems

RoofFlat RF-1, RoofFlat RF-2 and RoofPitch RP-1 are made of anodised aluminium. The anodic layer is 15 µm on RF-1 and RP-1 and 20 µm on RF-2.

## Stainless steel fasteners

Aluminium systems use stainless steel fasteners:

- A2 stainless steel is used in systems rated up to C3.
- A4 stainless steel is used in systems rated C4 and above, because it resists chlorides from salt better.

## Mixing metals

Aluminium and galvanised steel parts are separated by plastic washers where they touch, to prevent galvanic corrosion.
