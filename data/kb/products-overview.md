# Mounting systems overview

Example Solar Ltd. offers six mounting systems. Each has its own document with full details.

| Product | Mounting type | Main material | Corrosivity category |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GroundFrame GF-1 | Ground, single post | Hot-dip galvanised steel | C4 |
| GroundFrame GF-2 | Ground, twin post | Steel with zinc-magnesium coating | C5 |
| RoofFlat RF-1 | Flat roof, south-facing, ballasted | Anodised aluminium | C3 |
| RoofFlat RF-2 | Flat roof, east-west, ballasted | Anodised aluminium | C4 |
| RoofPitch RP-1 | Pitched roof, rails and hooks | Anodised aluminium | C3 |
| CarportFrame CP-1 | Carport | Hot-dip galvanised steel | C4 |

## Choosing between similar products

- **GF-1 or GF-2.** Both are ground-mount systems. GF-2 is rated for C5 and is chosen for coastal sites within 10 km of the sea and for sites near chemical plants. GF-1 is rated for C4 and is used at other sites.
- **RF-1 or RF-2.** Both are ballasted flat-roof systems. RF-2 puts less load on the roof and is rated for C4. RF-1 gives more energy per module because of its south-facing tilt.

The final choice is made in the preliminary design, after the site survey.
