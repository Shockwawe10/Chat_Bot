# About Example Solar Ltd.

Example Solar Ltd. is a fictional company created for this demo. It designs, supplies and installs mounting systems for commercial and industrial solar power plants.

## What the company does

- Designs mounting systems for ground-mount plants, flat roofs, pitched roofs and carports.
- Supplies the mounting structures, fasteners and foundation parts.
- Installs the mounting systems and fixes PV modules to them.
- Inspects and maintains installed mounting systems.

## What the company does not do

- It does not sell PV modules, inverters, batteries or cables. The customer or their electrical contractor supplies these.
- It does not do electrical installation or grid connection.
- It does not work on private homes. The smallest project it accepts is 100 kWp.

## Experience

The company has supplied mounting systems for more than 400 MWp of solar plants. It works with business customers, developers and EPC contractors in three sales regions: North, Central and South.
