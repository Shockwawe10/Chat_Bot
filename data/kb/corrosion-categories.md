# Corrosivity categories

The corrosivity category describes how aggressive the air at a site is to metal. The company uses the categories from ISO 9223.

| Category | Corrosivity | Typical environment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C1 | Very low | Heated indoor spaces |
| C2 | Low | Rural areas with clean, dry air |
| C3 | Medium | Towns and light industry, more than 10 km from the sea |
| C4 | High | Industrial areas, and coastal areas more than 10 km from the shore with some salt |
| C5 | Very high | Coastal sites within 10 km of the sea, and sites near chemical plants |
| CX | Extreme | Offshore and splash zones |

## How the category of a site is decided

The engineer decides the category during the site survey, using the distance to the sea, nearby industry, the wind direction and visible corrosion on nearby metal structures. When the site is between two categories, the higher one is used.

## What the category means for the product choice

Every mounting system is rated for one maximum category. A system can be used at sites with that category or a lower one. The company does not offer any system for category CX.
