# Foundations for ground-mount systems

## Foundation types

| Type | When it is used |
| --- | --- |
| Rammed posts | The default. Fast to install, no concrete. Used when the soil passes the pull-out tests. |
| Screw piles | Stony soil where ramming is not possible, or soft soil where rammed posts fail the pull-out tests. |
| Concrete foundations | Carports, and ground-mount sites where neither posts nor piles are possible. |

Rammed posts are usually driven 1.5 m to 2.5 m deep. The exact depth comes from the geotechnical survey.

## Geotechnical survey

Every ground-mount project needs a geotechnical survey before the detailed design. It includes:

- soil drilling to describe the soil layers,
- pull-out tests on sample posts,
- a check for groundwater and frost depth.

There are at least 3 pull-out tests per site and at least 1 per MWp of plant size.

The survey is ordered by the company from a partner laboratory. Its cost is part of the commercial offer.

## Earthworks

GF-1 can follow slopes of up to 15% and GF-2 up to 10% without earthworks. On steeper sites the company recommends grading the land before installation. Grading is not part of the company's services.
