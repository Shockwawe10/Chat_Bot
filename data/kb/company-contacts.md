# Contacts and working hours

## Email

- New projects and quotes: sales@example.com
- Existing projects and warranty claims: support@example.com

The company does not publish a phone number. A project manager calls the customer back after the first email if a call is useful.

## Working hours

Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00. The company is closed on weekends and public holidays.

Emails received outside working hours are answered on the next working day. The usual reply time is within 2 working days.

## Regional managers

Each sales region (North, Central and South) has its own project manager. The sales team assigns the manager after the first request, based on the project location.
