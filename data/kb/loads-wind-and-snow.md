# Wind load and snow load

Every mounting system is calculated for the wind load and snow load at the specific site, to the Eurocodes EN 1991-1-4 (wind) and EN 1991-1-3 (snow).

## Maximum values per product

| Product | Maximum wind speed | Maximum snow load |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GroundFrame GF-1 | 32 m/s | 2.4 kN/m² |
| GroundFrame GF-2 | 36 m/s | 3.5 kN/m² |
| RoofFlat RF-1 | 28 m/s | 2.0 kN/m² |
| RoofFlat RF-2 | 30 m/s | 2.4 kN/m² |
| RoofPitch RP-1 | 30 m/s | 3.0 kN/m² |
| CarportFrame CP-1 | 30 m/s | 2.4 kN/m² |

The maximum wind speed is the basic wind velocity from EN 1991-1-4. The maximum snow load is the characteristic snow load on the ground.

## When a site exceeds the maximum

If the site values are higher than the product maximum, the engineering team either designs a reinforced version (shorter spans, more posts or more ballast) or proposes another product. This decision is made in the preliminary design.

## Where the site values come from

The engineer takes the values from the national annex of the Eurocode for the site location and checks local conditions during the site survey, for example a hilltop or an open field near the coast.
