# Site survey

A site survey is a visit by an engineer to collect the data needed for design.

## When it happens

The survey is usually scheduled within 10 working days of the request. The customer confirms the date before the visit is booked.

## What the engineer checks

- Site boundaries, access for trucks and machines, and storage space.
- Shading from buildings, trees and terrain.
- For roofs: roof type, slope, condition of the waterproofing, and access to the roof.
- For ground-mount sites: terrain slope, drainage and signs of stony soil.
- Distance to the sea and to industrial plants, to decide the corrosivity category of the site.

## Cost

The site survey is free for projects of 100 kWp and above that are in one of the three sales regions. For sites outside the sales regions, the company may charge a travel fee, which is stated before the visit is booked.

## After the survey

The engineer writes a survey report. It is the basis for the preliminary design and the commercial offer. For ground-mount sites, the report also says where the geotechnical survey will be done.
