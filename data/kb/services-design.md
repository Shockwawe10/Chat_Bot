# Design and engineering

Design turns site data into a mounting system that can be built.

## What the design includes

- Layout of rows or roof fields, with the module positions.
- Structural calculation for the wind load and snow load at the site.
- Choice of foundation for ground-mount projects, based on the geotechnical survey.
- Assembly drawings and a bill of materials.

## Two design steps

1. **Preliminary design.** Made after the site survey. It gives the layout, the chosen mounting system and the plant size in kWp. The commercial offer is based on it.
2. **Detailed design.** Made after the contract is signed. It includes the full structural calculation and the drawings used for supply and installation.

## Standards

Wind load and snow load are calculated to the Eurocodes: EN 1991-1-4 for wind and EN 1991-1-3 for snow.

## Changes after approval

The customer approves the detailed design before production starts. Changes after approval, such as a different module model, may require a new calculation and can change the price and the schedule.
