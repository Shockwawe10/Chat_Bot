# Roof assessment

Before a roof project goes ahead, a structural engineer checks that the roof can carry the extra load.

## What is checked

- Load capacity of the roof structure against the added weight of the mounting system, the modules and the ballast.
- Condition of the waterproofing.
- Drainage: the mounting system must not block water flow to drains.
- Fire access paths and distances to roof edges.

## Who does it

The customer provides roof drawings or an existing structural report. If neither exists, the company can order a roof assessment from a partner engineer at the customer's cost. The cost is stated in the commercial offer.

## Old waterproofing

If the waterproofing has less than 10 years of expected life left, the company recommends replacing it before installation. Replacing it later means removing the whole solar plant.

## Result

The assessment gives a written yes or no and any conditions, for example a lower ballast weight or a smaller area. Roof installation does not start without a positive assessment.
