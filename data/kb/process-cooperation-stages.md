# How a project works, step by step

Every project goes through the same stages.

1. **Request.** The customer sends the project data to sales@example.com. See the document about what to provide for a quote.
2. **Site survey.** An engineer visits the site. For ground-mount projects, a geotechnical survey follows.
3. **Preliminary design and commercial offer.** The company proposes a layout and a mounting system and sends a commercial offer.
4. **Contract.** The customer and the company sign a contract with the scope, price and dates.
5. **Detailed design.** The engineering team prepares the structural calculation and drawings. The customer approves them.
6. **Supply.** Parts are produced and delivered to the site.
7. **Installation.** Crews build the mounting system and mount the modules.
8. **Handover.** The customer signs the handover certificate. The warranty starts on this date.

## Typical durations

| Stage | Typical duration |
| --- | --- |
| Site survey | Within 10 working days of the request |
| Preliminary design and commercial offer | Within 15 working days after the site survey |
| Detailed design | 3 to 6 weeks after the contract |
| Supply | 6 to 10 weeks after design approval |

These durations are typical, not promised. The dates for a specific project are only those written in its contract.
