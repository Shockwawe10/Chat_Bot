# Prices and commercial offers

## No public price list

The company does not publish prices and has no standard price per kWp. The price of a mounting system depends on:

- the mounting type and the chosen product,
- the wind load and snow load at the site,
- the corrosivity category of the site,
- the soil and the foundation type,
- the plant size and the delivery distance.

Because of this, prices are not given by email, chat or phone before a commercial offer is prepared.

## How to get a price

1. Send the project data (see the document about what to provide for a quote).
2. The company does a site survey and a preliminary design.
3. The company sends a written commercial offer.

## Commercial offer

- The offer states the scope, the price and the payment terms for one specific project.
- The offer is valid for 30 days from its date.
- Prices of steel and aluminium change, so an expired offer is recalculated.
